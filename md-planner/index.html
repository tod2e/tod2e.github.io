Important

Generated ≠ automatically valid for every system

The planner generates transparent starting templates. Force-field-specific cutoffs, membrane pressure coupling, special restraints, virtual sites, free-energy settings, unusual water models and system-specific index groups can require changes. Always run gmx grompp, inspect warnings, and verify the MDP against the force field and the physical ensemble you intend to sample.

HMR: changing the timestep is not hydrogen-mass repartitioning. Atomic masses must be repartitioned in the topology beforehand.

Umbrella: each window still needs a starting configuration close to its restraint centre, usually extracted from a pulling trajectory or otherwise prepared.
