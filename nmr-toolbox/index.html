Conventions

Know what the calculator assumes

NMR software and pulse programs differ in whether point counts are expressed as real or complex points, how CPMG cycles are defined, and how indirect sampling is scheduled. This toolbox makes those assumptions explicit instead of silently guessing. For acquisition and dispersion work, match the definitions to the pulse program and spectrometer parameters you actually used.
