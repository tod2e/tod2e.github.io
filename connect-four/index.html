Options & rules

You go first. Scores are separate for each difficulty.

  1. Pick a column. Your disc falls into its lowest empty space.
  2. Take turns. Four in a row wins: across, down, or diagonally.
  3. A full board without a winner is a draw.

Keyboard: Tab to a column, then Enter or Space to drop. Use ← and → to move between columns.
