How to play

Read the room

1The psychic privately sees a hidden target on a spectrum.
2They give one clue that should land near that target.
3Their team moves the needle without seeing the target.
4The other team bets whether the target is left or right of the needle.
5Reveal: active team scores 2–4 for accuracy; the other team can earn 1 for direction.

This is an independently implemented spectrum-clue party game and uses no official artwork or card text.
