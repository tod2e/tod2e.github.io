    Design note

    BioCalc is intentionally extensible

    The generic formulations live in recipes.js. Custom recipes are stored in your browser. Once your lab protocols and theses are supplied, the built-in library can be rebuilt around the exact recipes you use, including preparation order, stock sub-recipes, sterilisation notes, pH, supplements and citations back to the source protocol.
