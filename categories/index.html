    How to play & score
    1. Everyone writes one answer per category, starting with the round's letter.
    2. When time is up, read your answers aloud.
    3. A valid answer nobody else wrote scores 2 points. A shared answer scores 1. A blank or rejected answer scores 0.
    4. Judge disputed answers together and keep scores on paper. First to 30 wins, or choose a finish line.

    Agree on names, abbreviations, and whether titles may ignore “A,” “An,” and “The.” Themes use common starting letters. Your group judges the answers.
